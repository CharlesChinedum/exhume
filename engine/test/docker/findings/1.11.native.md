## Notes (added by hand after three invocations of the script)

- The script was invoked three times in total (the first two before a probe-output fix). The tables above are the third invocation. None of the three produced identical Part 1 results: the five full-file hashes were different in every invocation (15 distinct hashes out of 15), and class counts varied (for example exorcised 2678 to 2707, port-bug 8541 to 8581, reason-modernised 70 to 76 across the 15 discovery runs). Line count (16448) and forge's reported calls (16384) were identical in every run.
- Part 2 (about 1e-4) failed in every run of every invocation, with a shrunk sequence of 1 call. Run at which it failed, by invocation: 1st: 136, 136, 136, 136, 124. 2nd: 136, 136, 136, 136, 136. 3rd (above): 136, 136, 136, 124, 109. The value 136 is the most common outcome but not stable.
- Seed handling observed: `--fuzz-seed 42` is accepted for invariant tests. `FOUNDRY_FUZZ_SEED=42` is accepted and `forge config --json` shows `fuzz.seed = "0x2a"` for it, but it was also not reproducible. `FOUNDRY_INVARIANT_SEED` and a `seed` key in `[invariant]` are not part of the config (`invariant` has no `seed` key; `forge config` shows null and does not error), so they have no seeding effect; their campaigns behave like the unseeded ones. Runs with `--fuzz-seed` or `FOUNDRY_FUZZ_SEED` (about 5106 to 5122 match lines) cluster more tightly than unseeded runs (5074 to 5250 in the two samples), so the seed has some effect but does not make the campaign reproducible. The cause was not investigated.
- The 1.9 fixture only contains the 6e-4 variant (rcv[0] == 0); the 1e-4 variant (also rcv[1] == 0) was recreated in `risks/1.11-seed-determinism/Witness.t.sol`.
- The 6e-4 'rare' condition from 1.9 failed at run 2 in all 5 runs in every invocation.

## Outside-repo causes (checked by the user)

The user checked two causes outside the repo. Both point to non-determinism inside forge 1.5.1's fuzzer, not in our handler and not in threading.

- With `--threads 1`, seeded runs still differ, from line 19.
- With `dictionary_weight = 0`, seeded runs match for 97 lines and then differ at line 98, where only the fuzzed actor index differs.

A Docker run of 1.11 is not needed, because it would not change this conclusion.
